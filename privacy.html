Privacy Policy

EverMoore Services, LLC · Effective October 23, 2026

EverMoore Services, LLC ("EverMoore," "we," "us") is an exterior cleaning company serving Lubbock, Texas, offering window cleaning, pressure washing, soft washing, and gutter cleaning. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect from customers and prospective customers, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address, and service address.
  • Service details: the work you request, quotes, scheduling, job notes, and service history.
  • Payment information: the payment method used and whether an invoice is paid. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Text message information: if you sign up on our text message sign-up page, the name, mobile number, and time of sign-up you submit, plus a record of the messages we send and receive.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and schedule the services you request and to communicate with you about them.
  • To send service-related text messages, such as appointment confirmations, arrival reminders, invoice and payment links, and review requests.
  • To process payments, keep business records, and meet tax and legal obligations.
  • To respond to your questions and resolve complaints.

Text Messaging (SMS)

You can agree to receive text messages by checking the consent box on our text message sign-up page or by saying yes when one of our representatives asks while booking your service. If you agree, we use your mobile number only to send the service-related messages described above. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchasing any service.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Who We Share Information With

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business and only as needed for that purpose, such as payment processing, text message delivery, payroll, and secure data hosting. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

How Long We Keep It

We keep customer and job records for as long as needed to provide service, handle warranties and disputes, and meet legal and tax requirements.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including access-controlled business systems. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can also ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information by calling us at the number below. We may need to keep certain records as required by law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
